States the main screen cannot show at rest

Two things a real PvP game will hit constantly. Both are engine-backed, not invented for the mockup.

1 · Save & conflict surface

assertExpectedStateVersion rejects a write whose stateVersion is stale. With two managers acting near-simultaneously that is routine, not exceptional. It needs a permanent slot, because a decision that silently failed to save is the worst outcome in a game where decisions lock.

Rejection is safe — the engine refused the write, so no half-applied state exists. The message says that, because a manager whose click did nothing will otherwise assume the opposite and click again.

2 · Half-inning transition

On the third out everything on screen inverts — both rails swap teams, the field repopulates with the other nine, the batter card changes sides. Without an announcement a manager loses their place. Smaller than a big-moment takeover, and it must be dismissible rather than timed, because the other manager may still be acting.
